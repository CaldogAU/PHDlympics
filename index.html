          How country scoring works

          Multiple teams, one fair country score

          Every team competes and keeps its full result, but only each country's best team contributes to the Country Championship for that game.

          Extra teams never increase a country's point ceiling: a game with 14 teams representing 10 countries awards a maximum of 10 Country Championship points, not 14.

          1. Countries may enter multiple teams

            Teams are separate competitors assigned to a country. They continue to enter games, lobbies, matches and tournament rounds independently.

          2. Every team receives its real result

            The complete game leaderboard is preserved. Team placements, match history, statistics, medals and records are not removed or combined.

          3. The best team represents each country

            For each completed game, the highest-ranked team belonging to a country becomes that country's scoring team. Lower-ranked teams from the same country retain their results but add no extra Country Championship points.

          4. Countries are ranked again

            After duplicate countries are removed, the remaining countries receive new positions in their original performance order. This prevents gaps caused by non-scoring teams.

          5. The point pool matches the country field

            Last country receives 1 point and each higher country receives one more. Ten represented countries therefore award 10 points for first, down to 1 point for tenth, regardless of how many teams entered.

          6. The scoring team may change by game

            Different teams from the same configured country can contribute its best result in different games.
